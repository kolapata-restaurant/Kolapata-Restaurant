About Kolapata

A taste of Bangladesh & South Asia in Lisbon

Welcome to Kolapata Restaurant

Kolapata Restaurant brings authentic Bangladeshi and Indian flavours to Lisbon. Our goal is simple: delicious food, generous portions and a warm restaurant experience for everyone.


From traditional rice dishes and curries to crispy snacks and street-food favourites, we prepare our food with care and traditional South Asian flavours.
